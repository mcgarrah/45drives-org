# About this site

**45drives.org is an independent, community-built site.** It is not owned, operated,
authorized, or endorsed by 45Drives Inc. or its parent company, Protocase Inc. For official
product information, sales, and support, visit
[45drives.com](https://www.45drives.com).

## Why this exists

45Drives sells storage hardware and support built on a genuinely open-source software stack —
Ceph, OpenZFS, and a Cockpit-based management dashboard the company calls Houston UI. That's a
real, differentiated story worth telling well: most enterprise storage vendors lock customers
into proprietary software they can't inspect or run anywhere else. 45Drives doesn't, and in
our view that fact deserves clearer, more accessible explanation than it currently gets.

This site was built to do that: explain the open-source pieces on their own terms, show what
they look like in practice, and point people toward a real, hands-on example
([`ceph-gateway-45drive`](https://github.com/mcgarrah/ceph-gateway-45drive)) they can try
themselves.

## Who's behind it

This site is maintained by Michael McGarrah, a technologist who documented this stack firsthand
while researching storage-layer security go-to-market strategy for a graduate consulting
project. That research surfaced two things worth acting on independently of the original
project: first, that 45Drives' open-source story wasn't being told as clearly as it could be;
and second, that a closely related domain to 45Drives' own was sitting unregistered. Buying it
and building something useful on it seemed better than leaving it available for someone with
less good-faith intentions.

## A note on independence

Nothing on this site should be read as 45Drives' own official position, pricing, or product
claims — always verify anything that matters against [45drives.com](https://www.45drives.com)
directly. If 45Drives would like to be involved in, take over, or otherwise direct what happens
with this site or the `45drives.org` domain, that conversation is welcome — reach out via the
contact information on [45drives.com](https://www.45drives.com), or through the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/mcgarrah).
